设备上的数据 / Data on your device

登录凭证使用 Android Keystore 中的 AES-GCM 密钥加密后保存在本 App 私有存储中；退出登录、切换账号或会话经服务端确认失效时更新或清除。规划方案和您设置的屏蔽列表仅按账号保存在当前设备，不上传、不自动同步；举报作为私密需求提交给运营团队（见上表）。App 关闭了 Android 系统备份，卸载 App 会移除上述本机数据。
Sign-in credentials are encrypted with an AES-GCM key held in the Android Keystore and kept in the app's private storage; they are updated or cleared when you sign out, switch accounts, or the server confirms a session is invalid. Planning drafts and your block list are kept per account on the current device and are not uploaded or synced; reports are sent to the operations team as private requests (see the table above). Android system backup is disabled for the app, and uninstalling it removes this on-device data.

传输、存储与共享 / Transfer, storage and sharing

所有网络通信均使用 HTTPS（App 禁止明文 HTTP）。账号与私密数据发送到 app-api.icook.cloud；公开资料读取自 www.icook.cloud、bom.icook.cloud、export.icook.cloud、project.icook.cloud。服务运行在 Cloudflare 基础设施上，数据可能存储于美国及其全球节点。
All network traffic uses HTTPS; cleartext HTTP is disabled. Account and private data go to app-api.icook.cloud; public content is read from www.icook.cloud, bom.icook.cloud, export.icook.cloud, and project.icook.cloud. These services run on Cloudflare infrastructure, and data may be stored in the United States and Cloudflare's global locations.

私密需求及回复仅您本人与运营团队可见，不向供应商或其他用户公开。名录资料是您选择公开的内容。我们不出售个人信息，不将其用于广告；除法律要求外不向第三方披露。
Private requests and replies are visible only to you and the operations team, never to suppliers or other users. Directory content is what you choose to publish. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising, and we do not disclose it to third parties except where required by law.

资料服务可能保留必要的安全与运行日志；本 App 不将搜索建立为跨 App 的用户画像。
The catalog services may retain necessary security and operational logs; the app does not turn searches into cross-app profiles.

删除账号与数据 / Deleting your account and data

您可以在 App 内删除账号，也可以不安装 App 通过邮件申请，步骤与删除范围见 数据删除说明。
You can delete your account in the app, or request deletion by email without the app. Steps and scope are on the data deletion page.

未成年人 / Children

本 App 面向成年餐饮从业者，不面向 18 周岁以下用户，也不会有意收集其信息。
The app is intended for adult restaurant professionals, is not directed at anyone under 18, and does not knowingly collect their information.
