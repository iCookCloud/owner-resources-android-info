餐厅老板圈：删除账号与数据
Restaurant Owners' Circle: delete your account and data

开发者 / Developer：Better Day USA Inc. · Android 应用 / app org.zqnative.zqicook

方式一：在 App 内删除 / Option 1: in the app

  1. 打开 App，进入「我的」并登录要删除的账号。
    Open the app, go to Me, and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. 点击「注销账号」，在确认对话框中确认。
    Tap Delete Account and confirm in the dialog.

方式二：不安装 App，通过邮件申请 / Option 2: by email, without the app

使用账号注册邮箱发送邮件至 xu@icook.cloud，主题注明「删除餐厅老板圈账号」。为防止他人冒用，我们只处理来自该账号邮箱的申请，并会回复处理结果。
Email xu@icook.cloud from the email address registered to the account, with the subject "Delete my Restaurant Owners' Circle account". To prevent misuse, we act only on requests sent from that address, and we reply with the result.

删除后会发生什么 / What deletion does

  • 立即撤销该账号的全部登录会话；该邮箱此后无法再登录此账号，账号资料不再通过接口提供。
    All sessions for the account are revoked immediately; the email can no longer sign in to this account, and its profile is no longer served.
  • 该账号发布的内容与厂家名录资料下架，不再出现在公开列表、搜索或详情中。
    The account's published content and directory entry are taken down from public lists, search, and detail pages.
  • 该账号的私密需求及回复停止处理并不再显示；收藏失效；人才意向登记被删除。
    The account's private requests and replies are closed and no longer shown; its favorites are deactivated; its talent interest registrations are deleted.
  • 本机登录凭证随退出清除；本机规划方案在卸载 App 时移除。
    On-device credentials are cleared on sign-out; on-device planning drafts are removed when you uninstall the app.

保留的数据 / Data we keep

已删除账号的记录会被标记为已删除并保留，用于阻止该账号再次登录。下架的内容、名录、需求与回复以停用状态保留在数据库中，不再向其他普通用户展示；运营团队仍可能为处理请求与必要运维访问，目前不会自动清除。如需清除这些停用记录，请按方式二从注册邮箱发邮件申请。
A deleted account's record is kept, marked as deleted, so the account cannot sign in again. Taken-down content, directory entries, requests, and replies are kept in the database in an inactive state, no longer shown to other ordinary users; the operations team may still access them to process requests and operate the service. They are not currently purged automatically. To have these inactive records erased, send a request from the registered email as described in Option 2.
